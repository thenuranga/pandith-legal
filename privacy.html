Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 | Last Updated: November 20, 2026

Summary: Pandith uses your microphone solely for live spoken English tutoring and speech translation. We do not sell your personal audio data or share it with third-party advertisers. Your privacy is fundamental to our service.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Pandith ("the App", "we", "our", or "us"). Pandith is an AI-powered conversational language tutoring and real-time speech translation mobile application designed to help users gain spoken English fluency.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Pandith application on mobile devices.

2. Information We Collect and Process

  • Audio and Voice Data: When you use the Live Call Tutor or Live Translate features, the App captures audio via your device microphone in real-time. This audio is streamed in memory as short PCM chunks to process speech recognition, conversational understanding, and real-time translation.
  • Transcription & Practice Content: Spoken sentences and AI corrections generated during sessions are processed to show on-screen subtitles and allow shadowing practice.
  • Saved Phrases (Local): Phrases you choose to bookmark into your Saved Phrases Vault are saved locally on your device via secure app storage.
  • Account & Usage Data (Optional): If you create an account, we may store your email address, display name, and session duration metrics (such as daily minutes used) to manage subscription quotas.

3. How Audio Data Is Processed

Pandith uses high-performance generative AI models, including Google Gemini Multimodal Live API services, to interpret speech and generate authentic responses.

  • Microphone audio is transmitted over encrypted, secure WebSocket connections (WSS / TLS).
  • We do not permanently archive, store, or sell your raw conversational voice recordings on public or advertising servers.
  • Audio is buffered transiently in volatile device memory only for the duration needed to stream translation and playback.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with trusted enterprise cloud infrastructure providers to run Pandith:

  • Google Gemini AI (Google Cloud): Processes spoken audio and generates natural language responses under Google Cloud enterprise data privacy terms.
  • Google Firebase: Provides secure backend authentication, application telemetry, and crash diagnostics.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain account metrics only as long as your account remains active. You hold complete ownership and control over your data:

  • Local Data: You can clear your Saved Phrases Vault and translation history directly inside the App settings at any time.
  • Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your Pandith account and all associated profile data anytime by going to Settings > Delete Account inside the App, or by submitting a deletion request via our support email.

6. Children's Privacy

Pandith is intended for general language learners and students. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 without verified parental consent.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
