Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 | Last Updated: November 20, 2026

Summary: Pandith uses your microphone solely for spoken English coaching and instant translation. We never sell your voice recordings or share audio data with third-party advertisers.

1. Overview

Welcome to Pandith ("we", "our", or "us"). Pandith is an AI-powered conversational language companion designed to help learners gain spoken English fluency through interactive live coaching and real-time speech translation.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, and protect your information when using the Pandith mobile application.

2. Information We Process

  • Voice and Audio Data: When actively using Live Call or Live Translate, Pandith captures microphone audio in short PCM chunks via secure WebSockets to process speech recognition and generate spoken responses.
  • Practice Subtitles: Transcriptions of spoken utterances are processed in memory to display subtitles, corrections, and translations on your screen.
  • Saved Phrases Vault: Phrases you choose to bookmark are stored locally on your device storage (SharedPreferences) for off-line personal review.
  • Account Credentials: If you sign in, your email address and authentication tokens are managed securely through Google Firebase Authentication.

3. How Audio Data Is Handled

Pandith communicates with generative AI services over encrypted Transport Layer Security (TLS/WSS):

  • Voice audio is streamed transiently in volatile device memory only for the duration required to process speech.
  • We do not permanently archive, retain, or store your voice audio on public servers or advertising trackers.
  • Audio data is never used to build advertising profiles or sold to data brokers.

4. Third-Party Services

Pandith utilizes trusted infrastructure to run tutoring and speech features:

  • Google Cloud & Gemini AI: Performs real-time speech processing and natural language generation under Google Cloud enterprise terms.
  • Google Firebase: Manages secure user authentication and crash telemetry.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You maintain full control of your personal data at all times:

  • Local Data: Clear your translation history and saved phrases vault anytime from inside the app.
  • Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and profile data anytime by tapping Settings > Delete Account in the app, or by contacting our support team.

6. Contact Us

For any privacy questions or requests regarding Pandith, please contact us:

  • Email: support@pandith.lk (or pandith.app.help@gmail.com)
  • In-App: Contact Us form in Settings
