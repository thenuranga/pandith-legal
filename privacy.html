Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 | Last Updated: November 20, 2026

Summary: Pandith captures audio via your microphone exclusively to provide spoken English coaching and instant translation. We never sell your voice recordings or share personal audio with third-party advertisers.

1. Overview

Welcome to Pandith ("we", "our", or "us"). Pandith is an AI-powered spoken language companion designed to help learners achieve conversational English fluency through real-time AI live call tutoring and simultaneous speech translation.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, handle, and protect your information when you use the Pandith mobile app.

2. Information We Process

  • Voice and Audio Data: When actively using Live Call or Live Translate, Pandith streams PCM microphone audio chunks via encrypted WebSockets to process your speech and generate real-time voice feedback.
  • Practice Subtitles: Transcriptions of spoken sentences are processed in memory to display subtitles, pronunciation notes, and translations on your screen.
  • Saved Phrases Vault: Phrases you choose to bookmark are stored locally on your device storage (SharedPreferences) for off-line personal review.
  • Account Credentials: If you sign in, your email address and profile identifiers are managed securely via Google Firebase Authentication.

3. How Audio Data Is Handled

Pandith connects to Google Gemini Multimodal Live API services over secure Transport Layer Security (TLS/WSS):

  • Your voice audio is streamed transiently in volatile device memory only for the duration required to interpret speech.
  • We do not permanently archive, retain, or store your voice audio on public servers or advertising trackers.
  • Audio data is never used to construct advertising profiles or sell data to brokers.

4. Third-Party Services

Pandith uses trusted infrastructure to deliver high-quality language tutoring:

  • Google Cloud & Gemini AI: Performs speech-to-speech AI processing under enterprise Google Cloud confidentiality standards.
  • Google Firebase: Manages secure user authentication and crash analytics.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You maintain full ownership of your data at all times:

  • Local Data: Clear your translation history and saved phrases vault anytime from inside the app.
  • Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and associated profile data anytime by tapping Settings > Delete Account in the app, or by contacting us.

6. Contact Us

For any privacy inquiries or assistance regarding Pandith, reach out to us:

  • Email: support@pandith.lk (or pandith.app.help@gmail.com)
  • In-App: Feedback / Contact Us form in Settings
